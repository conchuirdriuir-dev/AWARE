# Biologist review: triage

The review (`biologist.md`) was written by a fresh Claude agent with a behavioural and sensory biology lens, given only
the design packet (`packet.md`). Triage by the builder, 2026-10-31, for the owner's decision; nothing here enters the
design until the owner decides. Verdicts: **adopt** (into the design now), **test** (add a toy condition or reading
first, and adopt only if it earns its place), **measure** (on the real model, like D), **defer** (a later capacity or
round), **refer** (touches a decision owned elsewhere; goes to the doc as a tension-log item).

**Convergences.** Finding 7 (news against the whole session) is the gap round 6's blind structure exposed, found
independently and by a different route. Finding 4's orienting-response bias is the review comment that moved δ after
the intake, now with a reason it may outlast the intake. Finding 5's stereotypy is round 5's blind structure, with a
harder variant (it responds briefly, then returns to its cycle).
